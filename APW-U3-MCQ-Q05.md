## Question

Which of the following best explains the process illustrated in the image?

## Answer Choices

- A. States increasingly relied on slave soldiers to establish large empires.
- B. The invention of the stirrup allowed expanding states to use cavalry more effectively.
- C. The development of new types of armor reduced casualties and allowed states to expand faster than before.
- D. States used gunpowder weapons to establish large empires.

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: D**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> At Panipat in 1526 Babur's army defeated the much larger Delhi Sultanate force using field artillery — large numbers of light guns served by combined musket and artillery units, supported by mounted archers and, crucially, by Babur's use of the tulughma formation to break and envelop the opposing line. This is the classic demonstration that gunpowder weapons allowed relatively small, well-organized agrarian armies to defeat much larger traditional forces, and it founded the Mughal Empire.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** Slave soldiers are characteristic of the pre-gunpowder Islamic world — the Mamluks, the ghilman, the Ottoman Janissaries in their earlier phase. Babur's force was not a slave army, and reliance on slaves worked against the large drilled infantry and artillery that made gunpowder empires possible.
> - **B.** The stirrup is associated with very early mounted warfare and is often linked to the early spread of feudalism in the first millennium. By 1526 cavalry tactics were centuries old and were not the decisive factor at Panipat.
> - **C.** New armour had no such effect. Advances in armour had marginal military impact in this period; the transformation in 1450–1750 came from artillery and firearms, not from metalwork.
