## Question

The two dynasties whose expansions are illustrated by the images shared which of the following?

## Answer Choices

- A. Their rulers claimed to be descended from the Mongol ruling family of Chinggis Khan
- B. Their rulers were recognized as caliphs by most Muslims
- C. Their rulers were descended from Turkic peoples of Central Asian descent
- D. Their rulers claimed power by virtue of protecting Dar al-Islam from European invasion

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> Both dynasties traced their origin beyond the settled world to Central Asian nomadic peoples. The Ottomans claimed descent from the Oghuz Turks, whose exploits were recorded in the Islamic historical tradition, and the empire's sultan was styled khan as well as sultan. The Mughals descended from Babur, who claimed lineage through Timur and Genghis Khan, and who brought with him the Turco-Mongol military tradition of the steppes. Both houses thus asserted legitimacy through a Turkic and Inner Asian ancestry rather than through conquest of China proper.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** The Mughals did claim descent from Genghis Khan, but the Ottoman dynasty did not. The Ottomans' genealogy ran through the Oghuz and through the gazi tradition of the frontier, and they held no Mongol lineage. The claim is therefore not shared.
> - **B.** Neither dynasty was universally recognized as caliph. The Ottomans gained the caliphate only in 1517, three centuries after the dynasty's founding, by inheriting the Mamluk position in Cairo; the Mughals never held it.
> - **D.** Neither ruler claimed power principally as a defender of Islam against Europe. The Ottomans fought Christians repeatedly and without such framing, and the Mughals conquered fellow Muslims — the Golconda image itself shows one — which undercuts a crusading rationale.
