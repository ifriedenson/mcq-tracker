## Question

The recruitment depicted in the image is best understood in the context of which of the following developments in the period 1450–1750 ?

## Answer Choices

- A. Attempts by imperial states to expand their economic power
- B. Attempts by imperial states to prevent religious conflict
- C. Attempts by imperial states to centralize their authority
- D. Attempts by imperial states to control population growth

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> The devşirme did two things at once: it built a standing army and administrative corps answerable personally to the sultan, and it deliberately removed promising boys from their families and hereditary local elites so that the Ottoman state, not the provinces, owned them. Office and command became the sultan's to bestow rather than a birthright of the notables. That is a direct expression of the period's broader drive by imperial states — Ottoman, Safavid, Mughal, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Ming — to bring administration and military force under central authority.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** Economic expansion was a goal of these states, but devşirme is not an economic measure. It extracted no revenue; it recruited personnel. Its purpose was to staff the state, not to enlarge the treasury.
> - **B.** Preventing religious conflict does not describe the system. The devşirme deliberately cut across religious lines, taking Christian children into a Muslim state's service, which is a means of overriding sectarian claims rather than avoiding sectarian strife.
> - **D.** Controlling population growth is not the object of a recruitment levy, and none of these states pursued population policy of that kind.
