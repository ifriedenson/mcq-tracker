## Question

Which of the following most likely describes the fate of the Golkonda state after 1650 ?

## Answer Choices

- A. It expanded to control most of India.
- B. It was conquered by the Portuguese.
- C. It was conquered by the Mughal Empire.
- D. It was conquered by the Ottoman Empire.

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> The answer is given by the source material itself. Image 2, on the very question this paper asks about, shows Mughal forces led by Aurangzeb capturing Golconda in 1687. Hyderabad, the successor territory, exists today precisely because the Mughal annexation ended the Qutb Shahi state. Aurangzeb's Deccan campaigns absorbed Golconda in 1687 and Bijapur three years later, bringing the peninsula under Mughal authority.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** Golconda was a regional power of the Deccan, never close to controlling most of India. Its rival neighbour to the north was expanding to absorb it.
> - **B.** The Portuguese held coastal enclaves in India — Goa, Diu — but never conquered Golconda or any interior Deccan state.
> - **D.** The Ottoman Empire never extended its power into the Indian subcontinent. Ottoman and Mughal rivalry existed at the level of diplomacy, not conquest.
