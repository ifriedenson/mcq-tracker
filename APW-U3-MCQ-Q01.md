## Question

Taken together, the two images best support which of the following claims regarding developments in the period from 1450 to 1750 ?

## Answer Choices

- A. European military technology was inferior to Asian military technology.
- B. Rulers served as military commanders and typically led armies into battle themselves.
- C. Gunpowder technology facilitated the expansion of land-based empires.
- D. Religious divisions were not a significant source of military conflict.

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> Both images show early-modern land empires taking fortified positions with the military technology of the gunpowder age: Ottoman troops in a Venetian-produced manuscript of about 1665, and Aurangzeb's Mughal army reducing the fortress of Golconda in 1687. The Ottomans, Safavids, Mughals, and Manchus are the classic “gunpowder empires” — agrarian states that converted artillery and small-arms firepower into long overland campaigns of territorial conquest. The images are visual evidence for exactly that process.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** The images show Asian armies prevailing, but nothing establishes a comparative assessment of European versus Asian military technology. A is a claim about relative technological superiority that two pictures of successful campaigns cannot support.
> - **B.** Aurangzeb is shown directing the siege of Golconda, but the Ottoman miniature is a manuscript illustration rather than evidence that Ottoman sultans personally led armies into battle. Several of these rulers administered from their capitals rather than commanding in the field.
> - **D.** The images give no evidence about religious divisions as a source of conflict. In fact Image 2 depicts a Muslim ruler conquering another Muslim state, which suggests that dynastic and sectarian rivalry, not indifference to religion, drove these wars.
