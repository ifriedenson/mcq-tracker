## Question

The two passages best illustrate which of the following continuities in world history?

## Answer Choices

- A. While some states sought to impose religious uniformity on their populations, others embraced religious syncretism.
- B. While some states used religion to legitimize their power, others used military or bureaucratic means.
- C. While some states were willing to tolerate diversity within their territories, others suppressed diversity.
- D. While some states allowed for numerous official religions, others recognized only one official religion.

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> Source 1, the Castilian law code of Alfonso X, protects Jewish worship, restricts forced conversion, and guarantees Jewish legal standing — a policy of toleration. Source 2, the decree of Ferdinand and Isabella, orders the expulsion of the Jews — a policy of suppression. The two documents together illustrate a genuine continuity across the period: rulers dividedly, some governing plural religious communities and others expelling them. Both policies are visible within a single Iberian tradition only decades apart.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** Religious syncretism — the blending of traditions — is not what either passage shows. Source 1 permits distinct communities to practise their own faiths separately; it does not merge them, and Source 2 has no syncretic content at all.
> - **B.** Both passages rest squarely on religion, not on military or bureaucratic administration. Source 1 is a religious law code and Source 2 a religious expulsion decree, so the contrast the question asks about is not between religious and secular legitimation.
> - **D.** Neither passage describes a state with numerous official religions alongside a state with exactly one. The contrast is between tolerating an existing religious diversity and erasing it, not between different numbers of official faiths.
