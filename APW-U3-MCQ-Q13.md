## Question

The attitude toward religious practice expressed in Source 2 was most directly apparent in which of the following Spanish policies in the Americas in the period circa 1500–1750 ?

## Answer Choices

- A. The establishment of the casta system
- B. The state sponsorship of Jesuit missions to native populations
- C. The use of the encomienda system
- D. The development of Candomblé and Voudun among African slave populations

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: B**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> Source 2 reflects a conviction that Spain's Catholics should be protected from error and that conversion should be pursued by the faithful. The clearest American expression of that attitude was the state's sponsorship of Jesuit missions, which converted or attempted to convert Indigenous communities and then reorganised those communities around parish institutions. The mission system was the institutional form of the same conversionist impulse that produced the 1492 decree.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** The casta system was a social and racial classification of colonial society. It articulated hierarchies rather than promoting conversion, and it is not an expression of the religious attitude in Source 2.
> - **C.** The encomienda system was a labour and tribute arrangement that let colonists draw Indigenous labour. It was an economic institution, not a religious programme.
> - **D.** Candomblé and Voodoo developed in African and Afro-descended communities, largely outside or despite Spanish authority. They represent religious persistence and syncretism, the opposite of an attempt to impose uniformity.
