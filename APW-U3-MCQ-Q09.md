## Question

Which of the following most strongly contributed to the shift of power from nomadic empires to settled states that Darwin identifies in the second paragraph?

## Answer Choices

- A. The development and spread of gunpowder weapons across Eurasia
- B. The development of new shipbuilding and navigational techniques
- C. The spread of epidemic diseases across Eurasia, such as the Black Death
- D. The increased recruitment of nomadic soldiers by large agricultural states

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: A**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> The reason settled agrarian states displaced nomadic empires was a change in the balance of force. Cavalry-based steppe armies depended on mobility and grazing; settled states with large revenue could purchase or cast artillery, drill musket infantry, and sustain long sieges. Wherever settled states applied that firepower — the Ottomans defeating the Timurids, the Mughals destroying the Sultanate of Delhi, the Ming driving out the Mongols — the nomadic polity lost. Gunpowder, not plague, shipbuilding, or recruiting nomads as soldiers, is what produced this shift.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **B.** Shipbuilding and navigation mattered enormously, but they are what shifted power to maritime empires in the western Mediterranean and the Indian Ocean. They do not explain the specific transfer of power from nomadic empires to settled continental states that Darwin identifies.
> - **C.** The Black Death devastated both nomadic and settled populations, and its effects on Eurasian states are more visible in the later period. It was not the mechanism by which gunpowder states overcame nomads.
> - **D.** Recruiting nomadic soldiers strengthened existing states; it did not transfer power from nomads to settlers. In fact the settled states that won against nomads did so partly by drawing nomadic contingents into their own service and subjecting them to drill and artillery support.
