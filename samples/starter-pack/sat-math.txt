Q: If 2x + 7 = 19, what is the value of x?
A. 3
B. 6
C. 12
D. 13
Answer: B
Why: Subtract 7 from both sides to get 2x = 12, then divide by 2 to get x = 6. Answer D comes from subtracting 7 from one side only, which leaves 2x = 19.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Linear equations
Difficulty: easy

Q: What is the slope of the line that passes through the points (2, 3) and (6, 11)?
A. 1/2
B. 2
C. 4
D. 8
Answer: B
Why: Slope is the change in y over the change in x, so (11 - 3) / (6 - 2) = 8 / 4 = 2. Answer D is just the change in y, which forgets to divide by the change in x.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Linear equations
Difficulty: easy

Q: A jacket costs $40 and is discounted by 25%. What is the sale price, in dollars?
Answer: 30
Why: 25% of 40 is 0.25 × 40 = 10, and 40 - 10 = 30. Answering 10 is the discount, not the price.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Percentages
Difficulty: easy

Q: If f(x) = 3x^2 - 2, what is f(4)?
Answer: 46
Why: f(4) = 3(4^2) - 2 = 3(16) - 2 = 48 - 2 = 46. Squaring before multiplying gives 48 rather than 144, and the answer 46 is what follows.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Functions
Difficulty: medium

Q: Which expression is equivalent to (x + 3)(x - 5)?
A. x^2 - 2x - 15
B. x^2 + 2x - 15
C. x^2 - 2x + 15
D. x^2 - 8x - 15
Answer: A
Why: Expanding gives x^2 - 5x + 3x - 15, and -5x + 3x = -2x, so the result is x^2 - 2x - 15. Answer B gets the sign of the middle term wrong, and answer D adds the coefficients instead of combining -5 and 3.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Quadratics
Difficulty: medium

Q: What is the mean of 4, 7, 9 and 10?
Answer: 7.5
Why: The four values add to 30, and 30 / 4 = 7.5. The median is 8, so answering 8 means the middle value was found instead of the mean.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Statistics
Difficulty: easy

Q: A circle has a radius of 5. What is its area?
A. 10π
B. 25π
C. 50π
D. 100π
Answer: B
Why: Area is πr^2, so π × 5^2 = 25π. Answer A is the circumference, 2πr, and answer C doubles the area by mistake.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Circles
Difficulty: easy

Q: What is 3/4 of 60?
Answer: 45
Why: One quarter of 60 is 15, so three quarters is 45. Answering 80 multiplies by 4/3 instead of 3/4.

Q: Which value of x makes the inequality 4x - 8 > 12 true?
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6
Answer: D
Why: Add 8 to both sides to get 4x > 20, then divide by 4 to get x > 5, so 6 is the only listed value that works. Substituting 5 gives 12 > 12, which is false, and that strict inequality is the trap.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Inequalities
Difficulty: medium

Q: A price rises from $80 to $100. What is the percent increase?
A. 20%
B. 25%
C. 80%
D. 125%
Answer: B
Why: The increase is 20, and 20 / 80 = 0.25, which is 25%. Answer A divides by the new price instead of the original, and 125% is the new price as a percentage of the old one, not the increase.
Course: SAT
Test: Math
Skill: Percentages
Difficulty: medium
