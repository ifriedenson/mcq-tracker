MCQ Tracker paste template — copy one block per question, paste with Ctrl/Cmd+P, "Quick add questions from pasted text".
Rules the importer follows (see src/quick-add.js):
- A new question starts at a line beginning "Q:" or "Question:".
- Anything before the first "Q:" line is ignored, so notes at the top are safe.
- A line of three or more dashes separates blocks and is ignored.
- Choice lines look like "A. text", "A) text" or "- A. text", letters A to H.
- Leave the choices out and the block becomes a short-answer (SPR) item, keyed by "Answer:".
- "Answer: A, C" makes a select-all item. "Answer: B" or the choice's full text both work.
- Unknown field names are ignored rather than breaking the block.
