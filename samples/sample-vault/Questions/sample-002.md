## Question

If 3x + 5 = 20, what is x?

## Answer Choices

- A. 3
- B. 5
- C. 7
- D. 15

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal answer
> **Correct Answer: B**
>
> Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15. Divide by 3: x = 5.
