## Question

In the sentence "The experiment was inconclusive, so the team repeated it," what does "inconclusive" most nearly mean?

## Answer Choices

- A. Dangerous
- B. Expensive
- C. Not decisive
- D. Carefully planned

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> Inconclusive means the results did not settle the question, so the team repeated the experiment.
