## Question

Which organelle is the main site of ATP production in a eukaryotic cell?

## Answer Choices

- A. Nucleus
- B. Ribosome
- C. Golgi apparatus
- D. Mitochondrion

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal answer
> **Correct Answer: D**
>
> Mitochondria use oxidative phosphorylation to produce most of the cell's ATP.
