## Question

A solution has a hydrogen-ion concentration of 1 × 10^-4 M. What is its pH?

## Answer Choices

- A. 4
- B. 6
- C. 10
- D. 14

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal answer
> **Correct Answer: A**
>
> pH = -log10[H+] = -log10(1 × 10^-4) = 4.
