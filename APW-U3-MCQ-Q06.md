## Question

The methods of warfare shown in the image were instrumental in explaining the territorial expansion of all of the following land-based empires EXCEPT

## Answer Choices

- A. the Safavid Empire
- B. the Qing (Manchu) Empire
- C. the Aztec (Mexica) Empire
- D. the Ottoman Empire

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: C**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> The question asks for the exception — the empire whose expansion the gunpowder weapons shown did not explain. The Safavid, Ottoman, and Manchu (Qing) states were all gunpowder empires that grew substantially through artillery and musket-armed infantry. The Aztec state was not: its expansion rested on a tribute system built on warrior prestige, elite military orders, and a long-distance alliance and marriage network, using hand weapons and a distinctive agrarian calendar system rather than gunpowder artillery. Its defeat came at the hands of an enemy using gunpowder, not through its own use of it.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** The Safavid Empire expanded substantially under Shah Abbas I, who reorganized the shah's Janissary infantry corps with European-style musket drills and field artillery and used both in his campaigns against the Ottomans, Uzbeks, and Georgians.
> - **B.** The Qing conquered Ming China, Korea, and large parts of Central Asia, doing so with gunpowder artillery, siege cannon, and musket-armed banners in the early and mid seventeenth century.
> - **D.** The Ottoman rise from 1299 to 1529 rested heavily on gunpowder: the great siege cannon at Constantinople in 1453 and disciplined musket and artillery formations under Mehmed II.
