# 1.287.1 — remove the Study agent scroll track

The 420 px chat captures exposed a 10 px horizontal scrollbar track between the
transcript and the composer.

## Cause

It was not transcript overflow. The closed mention picker kept its base
`display:flex` after `.qt-hidden`, so it remained a 10 px box in the panel flow
and drew its own scrollbar. The transcript measured `scrollWidth === clientWidth`
at 420 px.

## Fix

- `.qt-chat-mention-picker.qt-hidden` now sets `display:none`, so the closed
  picker leaves no box and no track; the open picker is unchanged.
- Removed the earlier transcript `overflow-x:hidden`, `scrollbar-width:none`
  and `::-webkit-scrollbar` suppression. The thread no longer needs a
  scrollbar-suppression rule, and wheel/touch scrolling is unchanged.
- Re-captured the empty, streaming and error states in light and dark at 420 px.
  The settings captures are re-confirmed and clean.

## Unchanged

Streaming and Stop, copy/regenerate and message actions, model selection,
note/flashcard/test drafts with review-before-write, focus visibility, ARIA
labels, tab order, reduced motion, and the Obsidian-variable-only palette.
