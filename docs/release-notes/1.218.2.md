# MCQ Tracker 1.218.2

Fixes where new form fields land on pages whose visible area does not start at
0,0 — pages with an offset MediaBox, or a CropBox that trims the page. The box
check compared the request with the page's *size* only, so a field asked for at
0,0 on an offset page was accepted and placed outside what the reader can see.
The one-point border pdf-lib draws around a widget could also push a page-filling
box past the edge.

## What changed

`addFormField` measures the requested box against `page.getCropBox()` — the
visible page, origin included — and the refusal names that area and its origin
when the box does not fit. After the widget exists, its stored rectangle (every
ring, for a radio group) is checked against the same visible area; a box with no
room for the border is removed again and refused with a message asking for a
point of margin, so a refused request leaves nothing behind.

## Verification

`tests/pdf-editor.test.js` adds "a new field is measured against the visible
page, not just its size": on a page with a MediaBox at 20,20 a box at 0,0 is
refused as outside the visible area while the same box at 40,40 is accepted, a
page-filling box is refused for leaving no room for the border, and only the
usable field remains.
