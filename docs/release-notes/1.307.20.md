# 1.307.20 — Imported PDF text is cleaned before a model reads it

Released November 18, 2026. Obsidian 1.15.1.

## Why

Exam and textbook PDFs carry content that is not part of the exam: a distributor's QR block and its
"scan to open" caption, a download stamp, a watermark id, a running header and title, a copyright
footer, a printed page number, and the cover block a host site prints at the top of page 1. All of it
was passed to the model as document text, so a note could pick up a stamp as a source line, a URL as a
question, or `Page 7 of 21` as an answer choice, and a QR code could be cropped into the vault as a
figure in the middle of a question.

This release removes that text in three passes — a printed symbol and its caption, repeated page
furniture, then the first page's cover — so the AI prompt, `page.text`, the note bodies and the
Quizlet table reader all see the same cleaned page.

**On the version number.** The three passes were written as 1.307.17, 1.307.18 and 1.307.19 on the
PDF-import branch. Those numbers are already published from another line (QR code, PDF reading order,
whole-vault import proof), so the three ship together here as 1.307.20 and their content is unchanged.

## What changed

### A printed symbol and its caption are skipped

- **`qrSignature` (`src/pdf.js`)** reads the rendered pixels of a candidate figure: the patch must be
  near-square (0.72–1.38, so a bar-code strip is not one), 3.5%–45% of the page width, mostly
  near-black and near-white (mid-grey under 22%, which keeps photos, shaded maps and gradients), a
  dark share of 22%–78%, and it must change colour at least six times along its own middle row and
  middle column the way a module grid does. Nothing matching that is cropped into the vault.
- **`isQrScaffoldLine` / `withoutQrScaffold`** drop a "scan … to open/view/download/…" caption, a bare
  "scan me", and a line that is only a URL, from both the reading-order lines and the table lines.
- Solid logos, wide bar-code strips and photographs keep their place.

### Repeated page furniture is named from repetition

- **`repeatedFurniture(pages)`** calls a line furniture when a whitespace-and-case-insensitive copy of
  it appears in the same top (≤10% of page height) or bottom (≥88%) strip on at least
  `max(3, ceil(60% of pages))` pages. Nothing is keyed to a publisher's wording; a one- or two-page
  document returns an empty set.
- **`withoutRepeatedFurniture`** drops a named line only where it sits in that band, so the same words
  in the body of a page survive.
- **`isPageNumberLine`** removes `Page 12 of 21`, `12 / 21`, `- 7 -` and `page 3` only when the number
  is the page's own — a printed page number never repeats, so repetition cannot find it.
- The whole document is read before any page text is built, and both text copies are filtered with the
  same set.

### The first page's cover block is dropped

- **`withoutCoverPreamble(lines)`** reads only the leading lines of page 1, up to the first real
  question, source, document or heading (`isContentAnchorLine`), and never past twelve lines. From that
  preamble it drops only a line printed more than once on the page, a bare address or "visit
  example.com" line (`isBareUrlLine`), or an identifier — a long numeric id, a `WORD|1234` watermark
  id, a file name, or an id-leading slug (`isIdentifierLine`).
- A title, an instruction, a class heading, a `Name` field and the first question can sit in exactly
  that position and survive, because they appear once and are none of those shapes.
- `readPdfPages` applies the rule to the first page of a document with two or more pages and to nothing
  else, so a heading or file-name-shaped line in the body of page 2 is untouched.

## Evidence

A 21-page Unit 4 exam was rebuilt as a real PDF from its transcribed page text
(`artifacts/pdf-import/`, local; the repo keeps `artifacts/` out of git), with its host's furniture in
the same margin bands and its cover block on page 1. `readPdfPages` read it with production chunking
(`chunkPages(pages, 9000, 5)`):

| | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| characters on the 21 pages | 36,685 | 28,871 |
| download stamps | 21 | 0 |
| watermark ids | 21 | 0 |
| running headers / titles | 21 / 21 | 0 / 0 |
| copyright footers / page numbers | 21 / 21 | 0 / 0 |
| cover slug lines / taglines | 2 / 2 | 0 / 0 |
| question stems / answer choices | 31 / 120 | 31 / 120 |

Every one of the document's own lines survives verbatim. Page 1 begins at `Name`:

```
Name
Source 1: "[In the sixteenth through the eighteenth centuries] Europeans derived more profit …"
Andre Gunder Frank, ReOrient: Global Economy in the Asian Age, 1996
Source 2: "The societies of Europe had been at the margins of the great trading systems …"
David Christian, This Fleeting World: A Short History of Humanity, 2008
1. Which of the following best explains Europe's ability to gain a greater share of global trade in the early modern period?
A Easing of tensions among European states
B Adoption and improvement of maritime technologies by Europeans
C Europeans' increased interest in foreign languages and cultures
D Diffusion of European manufacturing technology and processes to Asia
2. The two interpretations of economic history of the early modern period differ most strongly concerning
```

The verifier computes page 1's expected line set by running the module's own cover rule over the
transcription, then fails on any other missing line, any surviving furniture or a missing `Name`
field. Its verdict: *every document line survives verbatim; no furniture, no QR artefact and no cover
block is left.*

## Tests

`tests/pdf-qr.test.js` builds real RGBA pages: a 25-module symbol beside a photographed diagram (the
photo is still captured), a solid logo, a wide bar-code strip, a symbol with a printed quiet zone, the
caption filter, and `readPdfPages` end to end. `tests/pdf-furniture.test.js` covers the repeated stamp,
a legitimate repeated line, the one- and two-page refusal, the body-position rule, the page-number
rule, the table lines, and five cover cases (a synthetic cover stripped, a single preamble line kept,
anchors stopping the preamble, the URL and identifier shapes, and a page-2 file-name line kept).

No note format, setting key, review schedule or network default changed.
