# 1.301.25 — The gear goes straight to AI settings

The Study agent's header gear called `plugin.openSettings()` with no page. That
opens the plugin's Global page on a fresh install, or whichever page happened to
be active last, so a learner clicking the gear from the chat panel did not reach
the AI controls the panel depends on. The error card already used
`openSettings("mcq-ai")`; the gear now uses the same route.

The final three audit rows also closed in this pass.

## What changed

- `src/chat-panel.js`: the **Settings** gear in the ChatControls strip calls
  `this.view.plugin.openSettings("mcq-ai")`. **Retry**, the error cards, the
  shared Settings routing and every other control are untouched.
- `tests/chat-panel-copilot.test.js` adds a focused test that clicks the real
  gear handler and asserts `openSettings` receives `mcq-ai`, so the destination
  no longer depends on the settings page that was open last.

## Verified live

Obsidian 1.15.1 was relaunched headless against the disposable vault with the
CDP port open and notice observers on the main and Settings windows. The vault
reported manifest **1.301.25**, and the copied `main.js` matched the freshly
built file.

- On the previous 1.301.24 bundle, a fresh launch, **Study agent: open the
  sidebar**, then the real header gear opened the Settings window with the
  plugin's `activePage` at **global** — the default Global page, not AI. That
  established the defect rather than assuming it.
- On the final 1.301.25 bundle, a fresh launch reported `activePage: global`,
  and the same click from the chat panel selected **MCQ Tracker** in the real
  Settings window and rendered **STUDY AGENT · AI AND THE ASSISTANT**, with
  **AI backend** and **Enable AI features** visible.
- The Settings window stayed responsive; no plugin notice fired and no new
  console error appeared after the click. A pre-existing, buffered Obsidian
  `kp: Request failed, status 403` was present before the click and did not
  repeat; it is not a plugin request.
