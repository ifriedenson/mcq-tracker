# MCQ Tracker 1.189.0

Calculus in the Reference **Graph** tab.

## What is new

- A **Calculus** section under Statistics with three fields and four buttons:
  - **f(x)** — the expression, in `x`, evaluated by the tab's existing compiler.
  - **from / at** — the derivative point, the lower integral bound, or the first range value.
  - **to** — the upper integral bound or the last range value.
  - **Derivative at a**, **Integral a to b**, **Sum a to b**, **Product a to b**.
- The result line shows the operation and its value, for example `Derivative at 3 = 6`, `Integral from 0 to 1 = 0.333333`, `Sum from 1 to 4 = 10`, `Product from 1 to 4 = 24`.
- Slider parameters defined in the graph tab's rows are available in the expression. Saved `f(x)`/`g`/`h` definitions and data-table columns are not: enter the expression directly.

## How each operation is computed

- **Derivative** is a central difference with a step scaled by the point (smaller steps could not change the result materially). A flat function gives exactly `0`.
- **Integral** is adaptive Simpson quadrature with a default tolerance of `1e-8`, following the scheme named in the plan row. No code was copied from `scijs/integrate-adaptive-simpson`; the implementation is local.
- **Sum** and **Product** walk the whole-number range inclusively. An empty range (`from > to`) sums to `0` and multiplies to `1`, and a single value returns that value. A term limit of 100,000 stops an oversized range.

## Edges and failures

- Reversed integral bounds return the negative of the forward integral; equal bounds return `0`.
- A derivative whose neighbouring point is not finite reports `The expression is not finite around x.`; an integral that touches a non-finite sample (a pole or a domain edge) reports `The expression is not finite between a and b.`
- A sum or product with a non-finite term reports the value's position, and a non-finite running total is refused rather than shown as `Infinity`.
- Non-whole range bounds, a negative or non-finite tolerance, a non-positive or non-finite derivative step, non-finite bounds and a missing or unparsable expression are refused.
- Every failure returns `{ok: false, reason, message}`, writes the message into the live result line and raises a `Notice`. Nothing throws.

## Scope and sources

- The plan row names `scijs/integrate-adaptive-simpson` (MIT) and `mathjs` (Apache-2.0) as references. Neither was copied and no dependency was added; the numerics here are local and covered by tests.
- This release does not symbolically differentiate or integrate, does not plot the integral area, does not accept fractional sum/product bounds, and does not remember the entered expression or bounds after the view is rebuilt.

## Still limited

- Numeric methods only: a derivative near a cusp or a discontinuous integral can be inaccurate even when every sample is finite.
- The calculus panel has not been exercised by hand in a live Obsidian vault, on desktop or phone.
- The cursor trace and hit-testing still cover explicit and fitted rows only.
