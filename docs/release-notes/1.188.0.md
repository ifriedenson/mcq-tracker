# MCQ Tracker 1.188.0

Statistics and list entry in the Reference **Graph** tab.

## What is new

- A **Statistics** section under the data table with three fields and one button:
  - **Values** — the list to summarise.
  - **x values** and **y values** — optional paired lists for a correlation.
  - **Calculate statistics** — computes and shows the result.
- Lists accept commas, semicolons and whitespace, so `1, 2, 3`, `1;2;3` and `1 2 3` are the same list. Signs and decimals are accepted (`-2.5`).
- The result line shows `n`, `mean`, `median`, `stdev`, `Q1`, `Q3`, `min`, `max` and `r`. A value that does not exist for the list (the sample deviation of a single value, or a correlation with no variation) is shown as `—`.

## Definitions used

- **Mean** is the arithmetic mean; **median** averages the two middle values for an even count.
- **Quartiles** use linear interpolation between order statistics: for `1, 2, 3, 4`, Q1 is `1.75` and Q3 is `3.25`.
- **Standard deviation** is the *sample* deviation, dividing by `n − 1`, and is undefined (shown as `—`) for a single value.
- **Correlation** is Pearson's r over the paired x and y lists. It needs equal-length lists with at least two values and variation in both; otherwise the panel says why and leaves `r` as `—`.

## Behaviour on bad input

- An empty or blank list reports `Enter at least one number.` and changes nothing else.
- Any token that is not a finite number, including `NaN` and `Infinity`, reports `“token” is not a finite number.` and no result is computed. Values are never silently dropped.
- Equal-length x and y lists are required; a mismatch reports `The x and y lists must have the same number of values.` Supplying only one of the two lists reports that a list is needed rather than guessing.
- Every path returns `{ok: true, …}` or `{ok: false, reason, message}`; nothing throws, and the message is shown in the live result line.

## Scope and sources

- The plan row names `simple-statistics` (ISC) as the formula reference. No code was copied and no dependency was added; the arithmetic here follows the standard definitions above, implemented locally and covered by the tests.
- This release adds no parsing of expressions into lists, no plotting of the entered list, no regression on the statistics lists and no persistence of the entered lists across a reopen.

## Still limited

- Statistics are computed on demand, not live as you type, and the panel does not remember its lists after the view is rebuilt.
- The cursor trace and hit-testing still cover explicit and fitted rows only; polar, parametric, implicit, point and inequality rows draw but give no readout.
- The statistics panel has not been exercised by hand in a live Obsidian vault, on desktop or phone.
