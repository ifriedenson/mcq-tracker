## Question

The inclusion of the image in the manuscript best illustrates which of the following features of the period 1450–1750 ?

## Answer Choices

- A. Rulers using art as a way to communicate with their illiterate subjects
- B. Rulers using art to expand their territories
- C. Rulers using art as a way to increase literacy
- D. Rulers using art to legitimize their rule

## Answer

> [!answer]- Reveal Answer
> **Correct Answer: D**
>
> ### Rationale
>
> The manuscript was produced to celebrate Suleiman the Magnificent's achievements, and it included an illustration of the sultan's recruitment of boys from his Christian subjects for service in the army and bureaucracy. Patronage of the visual arts in this period was a standard instrument of rule: court workshops produced textiles, ceramics, manuscript paintings, and architecture that broadcast the patron's power, piety, and magnificence to subjects and foreign visitors alike. Such art legitimated rule by displaying the ruler's person and achievements in a durable and impressive form.
>
> **Why the other choices are wrong**
>
> - **A.** The manuscript was a luxury object produced for the court and for diplomatic gift-giving, not a communication device aimed at illiterate subjects. Rulers in this period assumed their subjects could not be reached or swayed through images produced at court.
> - **B.** The image did not expand territory. It reported an achievement after the fact; the territorial expansion itself was carried out by armies, not by pictures.
> - **C.** Producing a lavishly illustrated manuscript did not raise literacy. Manuscript culture in the Islamic world was tied to calligraphy and high literacy among elite audiences, and displaying images was not a scheme to teach people to read.
